## User story

As a user three months and two draws into a protocol whose ferritin finally crossed into the functional range, I want my single most-improved marker named at the top of my Plan page — a calm, one-line "Ferritin came up from 32 to 48 ng/mL since February — now inside the functional range," sitting above the eat list and habit stack — so that every time I open the protocol I am reminded the work is paying off on a specific number, instead of having to drive to Progress and mentally diff two sparklines to remember which marker has moved.

## Why now (four lenses)

### Product Owner

The product already computes the user's single most-improved marker (`selectMostImproved` in `src/progress/most-improved.ts`, shipped with 0047) and already renders the moved-marker receipt at the moment of upload (0032, shipped, on `#/labs?id=N`) and an over-time share image (0047, shipped, as a downloadable PNG from Progress). What it does NOT have is a *resident* in-app surface that keeps the headline result in front of the user every day they open their protocol. The improvement card (0047) is a share artifact — it ships out, not in. The movement receipt (0032) lives on the panel-review screen and the user only sees it the day they upload. The Plan page — the screen the user opens *daily* during a protocol — surfaces the eat list, avoid list, habits, and the plan delta (0029), but never the body's response to all of that. The smallest meaningful unit of value is a calm one-line "headline" rendered at the top of the Plan page (above the existing eat list / habit stack / insights, below the eyebrow), exclusively when `selectMostImproved(panels, profile)` returns a non-null marker that has actually moved favorably. It reuses the SAME selection function 0047 uses (so the chosen marker is honest by construction and consistent with the share card), reuses 0032's voice helper or the same exact phrase format, and adds zero new computation. It is a render-time pull of two values into one editorial line on a page the user opens every day.

### Stakeholder

This is the **persistent timeline** moat operationalized at its highest-frequency touchpoint. The whole product exists to prove that the food-first protocol moves real numbers over time; today, that proof is buried (one upload, then forgotten until the next). Surfacing it on Plan — the daily-opened protocol screen — turns the moat into a constant, calm reminder that the loop is working. A chat box structurally cannot do this: it has no prior draw to diff against, no persistent plan to anchor the headline to, and no per-marker honest functional optimum to declare "now inside." It is the in-app sibling of 0047's share card (which exports the same fact outward) and the resident sibling of 0032's at-upload receipt (which only fires on upload day) — together they form the full retention loop around the single most credible thing the product can show a user about their own body. And it deepens the lock-in by reframing the Plan page from "today's to-dos" to "today's to-dos *plus the proof they worked.*" The selection's honesty is inherited from 0047's already-shipped logic — no new judgment, no new tuning, no second source of truth — so the line is true by construction and is omitted gracefully when no marker has moved favorably yet.

### User (at 7am on the phone)

I open Plan to check what I am eating today. Above the foods, above the avoids, above the habits, one calm sentence sits like an editorial dateline: "Ferritin came up from 32 to 48 ng/mL since February — now inside the functional range." Not a banner, not a card with shadows, not a confetti animation — just a line in the same type the eyebrow uses, before the protocol starts. The protocol is working on my number, and I am reminded of it before I scroll. If no marker has moved yet (because I am one panel in, or the next draw is still ahead), the line is just not there — Plan looks exactly as it does today. If my ferritin had not yet crossed the floor but moved toward it, the line reads "moving toward the functional range" — same calm, no spin. It worked offline, instantly, because the panels are already on my phone and the selection is the same one Progress's "Save the improvement" already runs.

### Growth

This is a retention ticket aimed at the protocol-resident user: the one whose churn risk is highest in the long valley between draws, when daily food and habits feel disconnected from any visible result. (Hypothesis: surfacing the user's most-improved marker as a daily headline on Plan raises the rate at which a user mid-protocol opens Plan a fourth, fifth, and sixth week running — because the gap between effort and observed result is what makes long protocols feel pointless, and a calm daily reminder that a real number moved closes that gap without nag or notification; back-of-envelope from how fitness products that surface a PR or streak headline on the home screen outperform those that bury it; treat as untested without remote analytics, which we will never add.) The "show me" frame is quieter than a share card but real: "every time I open my protocol it tells me my ferritin came up — it is the most motivating sentence I read all week" is exactly the line the persona uses on the friend who is curious why they are still following the plan three months in. It compounds 0047 directly: the user who sees the headline daily is the user who eventually taps "Save the improvement" and posts the card outward.

## Acceptance criteria

Each box maps 1:1 to a Playwright test scenario.

- [ ] `src/plan/headline.ts` (new) exports `composeMostImprovedHeadline(improved: ImprovedMarker | null, opts?: { now?: Date }): string | null`. Given a non-null `ImprovedMarker` (the SAME shape `selectMostImproved` from `src/progress/most-improved.ts` returns — `markerKey`, `name`, `unit`, `startValue`, `startDay`, `nowValue`, `nowDay`, `movedIntoRange`), it composes a single editorial line per the documented voice: into-range yields "<marker> came <up|down> from <start> to <now> <unit> since <start month> — now inside the functional range."; favorable-but-not-yet-in yields "<marker> moved from <start> to <now> <unit> since <start month> — moving toward the functional range." Returns `null` when `improved` is null. Pure: no DOM, no Dexie, no network. Asserted by passing a known into-range improved object and confirming the exact string; passing a toward-range object and confirming the toward variant; passing null and confirming null return.
- [ ] The Plan page (`src/pages/plan.ts`) calls `selectMostImproved(panels, profile)` (the existing function from `src/progress/most-improved.ts` — no second implementation, no second scoring function) and renders the headline near the TOP of the composed-plan layout — above the eat list, avoid list, habit stack, and insights, below the existing Plan eyebrow / plan-delta surface. Exactly one headline renders per plan view. Asserted by composing a plan on a profile with a clearly improved marker (e.g. a ferritin 32 → 80 pair) and confirming the headline sentence appears above the first eat row in the DOM order.
- [ ] The headline is ABSENT when `selectMostImproved(...)` returns `null` — i.e. zero panels, one panel, or no marker with a favorable move toward its functional optimum. On that case the Plan page is byte-for-byte unchanged from today (no empty heading, no orphan whitespace). Asserted on a single-panel profile and on an intake-only profile, confirming the headline element is not present.
- [ ] Direction word ("came up" / "came down") agrees with the sign of the favorable move: a rising favorable marker (ferritin 32 → 48) reads "came up", a favorably-falling marker (ApoB 110 → 84) reads "came down". Asserted with one rising and one falling marker, each yielding the correct direction word in the rendered line.
- [ ] The "— now inside the functional range" clause appears ONLY when `improved.movedIntoRange` is true (the start value was outside the functional optimum and the now value is inside it). When the move is favorable but the now value is still outside the optimum, the clause is "— moving toward the functional range." Asserted with one crossed-into-range pair and one toward-but-not-in pair.
- [ ] The marker's human display name resolves via `findMarker()` and follows the same `shortName ?? name`-with-sex-parenthetical-stripped rule 0047 uses (so the headline reads "Ferritin", not "ferritin_m" and not "Ferritin (Male)"). Asserted with a sex-specific most-improved marker on a male profile rendering "Ferritin".
- [ ] The `<start month>` token is the month-name of `startDay` (the same month formatting 0032's movement receipt uses on the labs review screen — reuse the same helper if one exists, otherwise document and assert the chosen format). Asserted by seeding a start draw on Feb 4 and confirming the headline contains "February".
- [ ] The headline matches Plan's existing editorial styling: ink/ink-soft, no oxblood, no emoji, no exclamation, no badge or chip styling — it is rendered with the same eyebrow/lede class family the Plan page already uses for editorial section headings. Asserted by confirming the headline DOM node carries the Plan eyebrow / lede class (or the documented new `.plan-headline` class which inherits ink/ink-soft and the editorial type tokens — pick one and document).
- [ ] The headline renders **zero Anthropic calls** and **zero network calls** — it is a pure render of local data (`allPanels()` and `findMarker()`) that the Plan page already reads. Asserted by composing the plan, opening Plan, and confirming the mock recorded no additional requests for the headline computation/render beyond the existing plan-compose call.
- [ ] The Anthropic key NEVER appears in the headline or any intermediate string. Asserted by seeding a sentinel key on the profile and confirming it does not appear in the headline DOM text.
- [ ] Renders on both **chromium and mobile-webkit**. At the 390 px viewport the headline wraps cleanly on one or two lines, does not push the eat list below the fold by more than one line height, and respects `min-width: 0` (the documented overflow fix). Asserted on both projects.
- [ ] Tour mode (0014) renders the headline against the tour's two-panel fixture if `selectMostImproved` returns non-null for the fixture (the fixture is already shaped to demonstrate a favorable move; 0047's spec confirms this). Shared-view mode (0017) does NOT render the headline — the shared payload has no panels/trend to honestly support the claim. Asserted in both modes.
- [ ] The existing Plan page surfaces — the eat list, the avoid list, the habit stack, the insights, the plan delta (0029), the share-with-a-friend action (0050), the protocol-card download (0033), the simulator (0019), the printable one-pager (0010), and the `compose` action — are byte-for-byte unchanged in behavior; the headline is purely additive and sits above them all. Asserted by exercising the existing Plan flows (compose, recompose, share, save card) and confirming each is unchanged.
- [ ] Privacy E2E (`tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts`) still passes — the headline is a local render with no inference; no new hostnames; no widening of the egress allow-list.
- [ ] Schema migration: **no** — the headline is a render-time transform of the existing `panels` rows and the existing `selectMostImproved` selection. Nothing new is persisted.
- [ ] New `tests/e2e/plan-headline.spec.ts` covers: headline composes the exact into-range / toward-range strings, headline renders above the eat list in DOM order, absent on single-panel and intake-only profiles, direction word for rising and falling favorable moves, sex-specific name resolution, zero Anthropic + zero network for the headline, the key never appears (sentinel), tour vs shared-view behavior, the existing Plan surfaces unchanged, mobile rendering.

## Out of scope

Explicit anti-goals. The dev agent will not do these even if they seem related.

- A second source of truth for "most improved." This ticket reuses `selectMostImproved` from `src/progress/most-improved.ts` verbatim. No new scoring function, no new normalization, no per-page tuning — if the share card (0047) selects ferritin, the Plan headline selects ferritin. Honesty by construction.
- A second voice helper for the move sentence. If 0032's movement-receipt voice (`moveSentence` or equivalent in `src/labs/movement-receipt.ts`) already produces an acceptable line for the into-range / toward variants, reuse it. If a small composer is genuinely needed, factor it into `src/plan/headline.ts` and keep it pure — never duplicate the phrasing across two files.
- An LLM-generated rewrite of the headline. The line is a deterministic render of two values; a Claude call would add cost, latency, non-determinism, and an egress to a feature whose entire value is being instant, local, and provably honest about the actual move.
- Attributing the movement to a specific food, habit, or supplement ("salmon moved your ferritin"). Same constraint as 0032: causation is not an honest claim from two draws. The headline states what moved, not why; the eat list below already names what the protocol is doing.
- A celebratory animation, color flourish, badge, or oxblood-on-into-range emphasis. The headline is one calm editorial line — the satisfaction is in the sentence, not the styling. No confetti, no green glow, no growing number animation.
- A "history of headlines" surface or a per-plan-version stored snapshot of which marker was most improved. The headline is a live render-time selection over the current panels; a versioned history of which marker was the headline on which day is a separate, larger design.
- Carrying the headline into the doctor PDF (0010/0016), the share link (0017), the Markdown export (0027), or the coach packet (0048). It is an in-app daily reminder, not an export artifact (0047 is the export artifact for this fact).
- A "promote a different marker" picker. v1 is the deterministically-chosen single most improved — same as 0047. A user-pick variant is a clean follow-up if it earns it.
- A version that fires on an *un*favorable move ("your ApoB rose"). The headline is a positive resident reminder; an unfavorable surface is a different design conversation (and risks reading as alarm). 0032's at-upload receipt already names away moves honestly at the moment they happen; this ticket scopes to favorable-only.

## Engineering notes

Files / patterns the dev should touch. Be specific enough that the dev doesn't have to re-discover the architecture.

- `src/plan/headline.ts` (new) — `composeMostImprovedHeadline(improved, opts?)`. Pure; receives the `ImprovedMarker` shape `selectMostImproved` already returns and composes the documented line. If 0032's `moveSentence` (or the equivalent in `src/labs/movement-receipt.ts`) already emits the exact into-range / toward strings, import and reuse it; only add a local composer if the phrasing genuinely diverges (e.g. the headline does not name the prior month exactly the same way). Document the reuse decision in the file's header comment.
- `src/progress/most-improved.ts` — read-only reuse. `selectMostImproved(panels, profile)` is the SAME function the Progress improvement card already calls. Do NOT duplicate its scoring, normalization, or name-resolution logic. If a small refactor is needed to export an additional helper for the start-month string, factor it into this module so the Progress card sees no behavior change.
- `src/labs/movement-receipt.ts` — read-only reuse of `moveSentence` (or whatever 0032 named its voice helper) where the phrasing matches. The two surfaces (at-upload receipt + daily Plan headline) should produce the *same* sentence for the same move; that is the consistency the ticket trades on.
- `src/pages/plan.ts` — in `renderPlan()`, after the existing eyebrow / plan-delta region and BEFORE the eat list, call `selectMostImproved(panels, profile)` (panels already loaded for the Plan page via `allPanels()`) and, when non-null, inject the composed headline into the Plan layout at the documented slot. The headline is one `<p>` (or `<h2>`-class lede), not a card. Do NOT add the headline inside the dashboard sub-grid where it would compete with insight chips — it sits above the protocol body. Tour mode reaches `selectMostImproved` through the same `allPanels()` shim 0047 already uses; shared-view mode skips the headline (the shared payload has no panels — guard with `isSharedView()`).
- `src/data/markers.ts` — read-only. `findMarker()` resolves the canonical name (`shortName ?? name`, sex-parenthetical stripped, matching 0047's rule).
- `src/types.ts` — no new persistent types; the `ImprovedMarker` type already lives in `src/progress/most-improved.ts` (or `src/types.ts` per 0047's note) and is reused as-is.
- `src/styles.css` — either reuse the existing Plan eyebrow/lede class (preferred) or add one new `.plan-headline` that inherits the same ink/ink-soft tokens, the Cormorant display family for the marker name, and Inter Tight body for the rest. No new colors. Grid children `min-width: 0` (the documented overflow fix). NO oxblood accent on the "now inside the functional range" clause — the headline stays calm.
- `src/sample/state.ts` / `src/share/shared-state.ts` — tour reaches `selectMostImproved` via the shimmed `allPanels()`; shared-view should not render the headline (the shared payload has no panels). Mirror 0047's `isSharedView()` gate.
- `tests/helpers/flows.ts` — reuse `seedPanel()` and the multi-panel seeding pattern 0047's spec already uses (a clearly into-range pair: e.g. `ferritin_m` 60 → 80 on a male profile, where the optimum floor is 70).
- `tests/e2e/plan-headline.spec.ts` (new) — every acceptance bullet maps to a `test()`. The composition test passes a known `ImprovedMarker` to `composeMostImprovedHeadline` and asserts the exact string. The render-position test composes a plan with a known improved marker and asserts the headline appears above the first `.dash-eats` (or equivalent eat-row) element in DOM order. The consistency test confirms the marker chosen on Plan matches the marker chosen on the Progress improvement card for the same panels (no second selection).
- `tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts` — extend with a Plan visit on a multi-panel profile confirming zero off-allow-list egress during the headline render.
- New deps: **no**.
- Egress allow-list change: **no**.
- Schema migration: **no**.
- Voice spec: into-range "<marker> came <up|down> from <start> to <now> <unit> since <start month> — now inside the functional range." Toward "<marker> moved from <start> to <now> <unit> since <start month> — moving toward the functional range." Declarative, no exclamation, no spin, no causation claim. Banned: "amazing," "exciting," "journey," "great." Ink and ink-soft only — no oxblood on the headline.
