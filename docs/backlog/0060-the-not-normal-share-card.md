## User story

As a user whose first panel just resolved and whose report card (0036, shipped) tells me "Of 22 markers, 12 are optimal. 7 sit inside the lab's normal range but below the functional optimum. 3 are outside the lab range" — the precise sentence I have been trying to say to my doctor for two years — I want a one-tap "Save this" on the report card that renders a phone-shaped editorial image of those counts (no marker names, no values, just the tally and the date), so that the friend I send it to sees the exact framing that made the persona's grievance legible, and lands one tap from the sample tour without me ever exposing a single biomarker.

## Why now (four lenses)

### Product Owner

The report card (0036, shipped) is the product's most persona-aligned line — it converts the founding wedge into an arithmetic fact on the user's own labs. It already renders cleanly on the post-extraction review screen. What it does NOT do today is leave that screen: the line is the most screenshot-worthy single artifact the product has ever produced for the "told everything's normal" segment, and the user has no first-class way to send it. A raw screenshot exposes the full results table beside it (every marker name, every value); a verbal paraphrase loses the editorial register and the date. The smallest meaningful unit of value is a "Save this" affordance beside the report card that renders a single, deterministic, phone-shaped image carrying ONLY the four numbers (total, optimal, suboptimal, out-of-lab-range), the eyebrow "Against the optimum", the draw date, and the wordmark — in the same Cormorant + Inter Tight + cream/ink/oxblood register the in-app card uses. No marker names, no values, no profile, no link. It reuses the marker-hero (0011) and improvement-card (0047) canvas-render-and-download mechanism verbatim, plus the existing `computeReportCard()` from 0036. The only new code is the layout. It turns the line the persona has been waiting to say into a shareable artifact without exposing any biology.

### Stakeholder

This compounds three shipped moat assets into the most forwardable acquisition artifact the product has yet built: the **functional-range DB** (the counts only mean something because the optimum is curated), the **report-card computation** (0036's deterministic tally is what gives the image its evidentiary weight), and the **structured artifact** moat (a saveable, editorial image lands in feeds where a chat-box paragraph cannot). A wrapper has no per-panel report card to count, no functional optimum to count against, and no render stack to compose a clean image of the result — "7 of my markers are normal but below the functional optimum" is structurally a sentence only a product with the curated DB and the dual-range classifier can produce on the user's own data. The acquisition channel is the persona's grievance translated into one image: the friend with the same "everything's normal" lab who has been waiting for vocabulary now has a sentence in editorial type, posted by someone they know. It is the marker-hero card's (0011) cousin aimed earlier in the funnel — 0011 shares ONE marker improving over time, this shares the ENTIRE panel against the optimum on the very first upload, which is the moment the persona's grievance crystallizes.

### User (at 7am on the phone)

First panel just resolved. The report card line sits above the results: "Of 22 markers, 12 are optimal. 7 sit inside the lab's normal range but below the functional optimum. 3 are outside the lab range." That is the sentence I have been trying to say. Beside it, a quiet chip: "Save this." I tap it. A phone-shaped image lands in my downloads, in the type I trust — "Almanac" at the top, "Against the optimum" below, then the four numbers in plain Cormorant, then the dated month — and that is all. No marker names, no values, no name. I post it to a story, or text it to the friend who is always tired and always "normal." She taps the wordmark search and ends up on the sample tour. I have not exposed a single biomarker; the image is the framing, not the biology. It worked offline, instantly, because the count is already on my phone and the render is canvas.

### Growth

This is the highest-leverage acquisition artifact the product can ship at the moment of maximum persona resonance — the first upload. (Hypothesis: a card that says "7 of my markers are normal but below the functional optimum" is forwarded friend-to-friend at a meaningfully higher rate than the single-marker card (0011) for the brand-new user, because the persona's grievance is "I was told I was normal and I'm not" — a tally framed against optimum is the exact answer-shaped version of that grievance, and it does not require the user to have a multi-draw timeline to share, so the artifact is available on day one rather than after a recompose; back-of-envelope from how "you have N issues your doctor missed" framings drive sign-ups in DTC lab products; treat as untested without remote analytics, which we will never add.) The "show me" frame is the image itself: a clean, dated, four-number card in editorial type is exactly what the persona screenshots and texts to the friend with the same complaint — and the friend lands one tap from the sample tour (0014) and one upload from their own report card. It is the at-rest, day-one sibling of the improvement card (0047): that proves the protocol worked over months, this proves the doctor's "normal" hid something on the very first draw.

## Out of scope

Explicit anti-goals. The dev agent will not do these even if they seem related.

- A new image / canvas dependency. The marker-hero card (0011) and improvement card (0047) already established the on-device render-and-download mechanism; this ticket reuses it verbatim. No html-to-image, no dom-to-png, no Puppeteer-shaped library.
- An LLM-generated caption, headline, or narrative on the image. The card's copy is a deterministic render of `computeReportCard()`'s sentence. A Claude call would add cost, latency, non-determinism, and an egress to a feature whose entire value is being instant, local, and provably honest about the count.
- Including marker names, values, units, or any per-result detail on the image. The privacy posture is the entire point: the persona shares the framing without exposing the biology. A multi-marker collage of "here are the seven markers below optimum" is a separate, more invasive artifact and is deliberately not this ticket.
- A QR code, URL, or any link on the card. We have no backend; the wordmark is the entire attribution. The friend who sees the card finds the (already-shipped) pre-key surfaces by searching, exactly as 0011 / 0047 / 0033 do.
- Customizing the card (font, color, hide-counts toggle, add-caption). The card has one layout. Configurability defeats the artifact discipline.
- A per-network "post to Twitter / Instagram" integration. The OS share sheet IS the integration. No per-platform SDK, no upload to a hosted destination.
- Carrying the image into the doctor PDF (0010/0016), the share link (0017), the Markdown export (0027), or the coach packet (0048). It is a social, top-of-funnel acquisition artifact, not a clinical or archival one.
- Surfacing the affordance on Today, Progress, or any other route. Its at-rest, first-upload immediacy is the point; an echo elsewhere is a possible follow-up, not this ticket.
