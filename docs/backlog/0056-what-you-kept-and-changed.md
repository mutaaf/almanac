## User story

As a user who came in by adopting a friend's shared protocol (0020) and then composed my own first plan against my own intake, I want to see a calm side-by-side of what my friend's pattern leaned on versus what my plan actually kept, dropped, and added — and a one-tap way to send that diff back to the friend who seeded me — so that I can close the loop with the person who brought me in ("here's what I took from yours and what was different for me"), instead of the referral being a one-way door that goes quiet the moment I set up.

## Why now (four lenses)

### Product Owner

The adopt-and-adapt flow (0020, shipped) is a one-way door: the friend shares, the recipient adopts, the recipient's first plan acknowledges the seed in one editorial line — and then the seed is cleared and the referral relationship goes silent. The recipient has, at that point, a concrete, interesting fact they would love to tell the friend: "I kept your magnesium and your walk, I dropped the cold plunge, and my plan added fasting-insulin support you don't have." Today there is nowhere to see that, and no way to send it. The smallest meaningful unit of value is a "What you kept and changed" surface on the Plan page, shown only to a user who arrived via adopt: a deterministic three-way diff of the friend's seeded pattern (the ≤5 habit titles + ≤3 eat foods 0020 already captured) against the user's composed plan's habit stack and eat list — kept (in both), dropped (in the seed, not the plan), added (in the plan, not the seed) — rendered as three short editorial lists. Beside it, a "Share back" action that pre-composes a plain-text message ("I adopted your protocol — here's what stuck for me, and what was different") via the existing native-share path (0050) and the existing shareable-protocol-link path (0017), so the friend gets the diff *and* a tappable link to the recipient's own protocol. It reuses the diff comparison the plan-delta surfaces already do (0029) and the share mechanisms already built; the only new logic is the seed-vs-plan set diff and the message composition.

### Stakeholder

This closes the **referral loop** that 0020 only half-built, turning the **structured artifact** moat into a two-way social channel without a backend or a single byte of cross-user data transfer — the diff is computed entirely from the recipient's own device (their seed, their plan), and the share-back rides the same hash-link + native-share paths the privacy contract already cleared. A chat box has no structural memory that the user arrived from a friend, no captured seed to diff against, and no protocol artifact to send back; "here is exactly what I kept and changed from the protocol you sent me, with a link to mine" is a thing only a product with a persistent timeline and a structured plan can produce. It deepens the loop precisely where referral programs usually leak: the moment after the referred user converts, when most products go silent. The recipient who shares back is also, in that act, seeding *their* friend with *their* protocol — so the diff is both the close of one referral and the open of the next, which is the compounding the moat depends on. To do this the seed must survive 0020's single-use clear: this ticket keeps a separate, diff-only retained copy (`Profile.adoptOrigin`) that is NEVER re-prompted into any LLM call — it exists solely to render the diff — so 0020's "the seed is single-use editorial provenance, not re-prompted" discipline stays intact.

### User (at 7am on the phone)

A week ago I adopted my friend's protocol and composed my own plan. On my Plan page now, a quiet section: "What you kept and changed." Kept: the magnesium before bed, the afternoon walk, the fatty fish. Dropped: the cold plunge (not for me). Added: fasting-insulin support, leafy greens daily — things my own numbers asked for that hers didn't. It is honest and it is mine. Below it, "Share back." I tap it; my phone's share sheet opens with a message already written — "I adopted your protocol. Here's what stuck for me and what was different, plus a link to mine" — and a link to my own protocol. I send it to her. The loop is closed; she knows what came of the thing she sent me. One tap, message pre-written, link attached, hands wet from the sink. It worked offline up to the share sheet — the diff was computed on my phone from data I already own; nothing was sent until I chose to send it.

### Growth

This is a referral-loop-closing ticket aimed at the highest-trust edge in the product's graph: the friend who already converted you. (Hypothesis: a share-back diff lifts second-generation referrals above what the one-way adopt flow achieves, because the share-back is sent to someone who has already demonstrated they value the product enough to share it — the warmest possible recipient — and because "here's what I kept and changed from yours" is a more specific, more flattering, more conversation-continuing message than a cold "you should try this"; back-of-envelope from how two-way referral loops compound where one-way ones decay; treat as untested without remote analytics, which we will never add.) The "show me" frame is the diff itself: a clean, typed kept/dropped/added comparison of two real protocols is exactly the artifact the seeding friend wants to see — it proves their recommendation mattered and shows them a tested variation of their own protocol — which makes them, in turn, more likely to share again. It is the loop-closing sibling of 0020: 0020 converts a friend's share into your plan, this sends your plan back as a tested answer.

## Acceptance criteria

Each box maps 1:1 to a Playwright test scenario.

- [ ] When a user composes their first plan via the adopt path (0020), a diff-only copy of the seed is retained as `Profile.adoptOrigin: { version: 1; habitTitles: string[]; topEatFoods: string[] }` — separate from the single-use `Profile.adoptSeed` 0020 clears after compose. `adoptOrigin` is set ONCE, at the first adopt-seeded compose, and is NEVER read by `formatProfile()` / any plan-compose prompt builder (it is render-only). Asserted by composing an adopt-seeded plan and confirming `adoptOrigin` is persisted while a subsequent recompose's prompt body does NOT contain it (reuse 0020's `lastPromptBody()` mock accessor).
- [ ] `src/plan/adopt-diff.ts` (new) exports `computeAdoptDiff(origin: AdoptOrigin, plan: Plan): { kept: string[]; dropped: string[]; added: string[] }` — a deterministic, case-insensitive set diff of the origin's habit titles + eat foods against the composed plan's `habitStack.habits` titles + `eatList` food names: `kept` = in both, `dropped` = in origin not plan, `added` = in plan not origin. Pure: no DOM, no Dexie, no network, no LLM. Asserted by passing a known origin and plan and confirming the three lists.
- [ ] The Plan page (`src/pages/plan.ts`) renders a "What you kept and changed" section ONLY when `profile.adoptOrigin` is present AND a composed plan exists. It shows the kept / dropped / added lists as three short labeled editorial lists. Asserted by seeding `adoptOrigin` + a plan and confirming the three lists render with the diff content.
- [ ] The section is ABSENT for any user who did NOT arrive via adopt (no `adoptOrigin`) — the overwhelming majority of users. The Plan page is byte-for-byte unchanged for them. Asserted on a normally-onboarded profile (no `adoptOrigin`) and confirming the section is absent.
- [ ] An empty diff facet renders honestly: if nothing was dropped, the "Dropped" list reads "Nothing — you kept it all"; if nothing was added, "Added" reads "Nothing new yet"; an all-kept diff still renders the section. Asserted with an origin fully contained in the plan (empty dropped, empty added).
- [ ] A "Share back" action sits beside the diff. Tapping it composes a plain-text message ("I adopted your protocol — here's what stuck for me, and what was different:" followed by the kept/dropped/added summary) plus a link to the user's own protocol built via the existing 0017 share-link mechanism, and invokes the existing 0050 native-share path (with the documented copy-to-clipboard fallback where the Web Share API is unavailable). Asserted by tapping "Share back" and confirming the composed message contains the kept/dropped/added summary and a `#/shared` protocol link.
- [ ] The share-back message and link carry NO labs, NO insights, NO marker values, NO biology — only the kept/dropped/added food and habit titles and the standard 0017 share-link payload (which 0017 already proved carries no labs/insights/profile). Asserted by composing the share-back from a plan stuffed with sentinel marker values and confirming none appear in the message or the link payload.
- [ ] The diff and the share-back message are computed with **zero Anthropic calls** and **zero network calls** (the share itself is the OS share sheet / clipboard, not a network egress we make). Asserted by rendering the section and composing the share-back and confirming the mock recorded zero requests.
- [ ] The Anthropic key NEVER appears in the section, the share-back message, the link, or any intermediate string. Asserted by seeding a sentinel key and confirming it appears nowhere in the diff section or the composed share-back.
- [ ] Renders on both **chromium and mobile-webkit**. The three diff lists and the "Share back" action (≥44×44 css px, min-height ≥45 px per the 0017 sub-pixel lesson) wrap cleanly at the 390 px viewport; long food/habit titles respect `min-width: 0` (the documented overflow fix). On mobile-webkit, the native-share path (or its documented fallback) is asserted per the 0050 spec's pattern.
- [ ] Tour mode (0014) does NOT render the section (the tour fixture did not arrive via adopt — never fabricate an adopt origin). Shared-view mode (0017) does NOT render it (the recipient is reading someone else's protocol, not their own adopt diff). Asserted in both modes.
- [ ] The existing Plan page (the protocol render, the plan-delta surface 0029, the tell-a-friend action 0050, the adopt acknowledgment line from 0020) is byte-for-byte unchanged in behavior; the diff section is purely additive. Asserted by exercising the existing Plan flows and confirming they are unchanged.
- [ ] Privacy E2E (`tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts`) still passes — the diff is local-only and deterministic; the share-back rides the existing 0017/0050 paths; no new hostnames; no inference call.
- [ ] Schema migration: **yes** — Dexie bumps to the next version to add the optional `Profile.adoptOrigin?: AdoptOrigin` field (additive; an existing profile loads with `adoptOrigin === undefined` and simply never shows the section). Migration test asserts a prior-version export imports with no profile field loss and the section absent.
- [ ] New `tests/e2e/adopt-diff.spec.ts` covers: `adoptOrigin` retained at first adopt compose but never re-prompted, the kept/dropped/added diff correct, the section present only with `adoptOrigin` + a plan and absent otherwise, empty-facet honest copy, "Share back" composes the kept/dropped/added message + a `#/shared` link, no labs/insights/values in the message or link (sentinel), zero Anthropic + zero network, the key never appears (sentinel), the existing Plan flows unchanged, tour vs shared-view absence, mobile rendering/share path.

## Out of scope

Explicit anti-goals. The dev agent will not do these even if they seem related.

- Re-prompting the LLM with `adoptOrigin`. 0020 made the seed single-use editorial provenance; `adoptOrigin` is a render-only retained copy that NEVER enters a plan-compose prompt. The diff is a deterministic set difference, not an LLM narration of the differences.
- A two-way live channel, a "your friend replied" surface, or any inbound notification. There is no backend and no two-way channel (0020's discipline). The share-back is a one-shot outbound message the user sends through their own OS; the friend receiving it is the end of our involvement.
- Diffing the friend's labs, insights, marker values, retest plan, or meal plan. The origin carries only the ≤5 habit titles and ≤3 eat foods 0020 captured (it structurally cannot carry more — the share payload never held labs/insights). The diff and the share-back are habit/food titles only.
- Naming the friend in the diff or the share-back. 0020's seed is anonymous by construction; the friend is the pattern, not the person. No name field, no "share back to <name>".
- Re-computing the diff against every later recompose so it drifts over time. The diff is the origin (fixed at first adopt compose) against the user's *current* plan; it updates naturally when the plan recomposes, but `adoptOrigin` itself never changes after it is first set. Do not re-capture the origin on later composes.
- An image/card render of the diff. The kept/dropped/added is a text section and a text+link share-back. An image-share of the diff is a plausible future growth ticket (sibling to the protocol card 0033 / improvement card 0047) but is out of scope here.
- Showing the diff to a user who arrived normally (no adopt). The section is strictly for the adopt cohort; fabricating an origin for a non-adopt user would be dishonest and pointless.
- Carrying the diff into the doctor PDF (0010/0016), the Markdown export (0027), or the coach packet (0048). It is a social, in-app, share-back surface — not a clinical or archival artifact.

## Engineering notes

Files / patterns the dev should touch. Be specific enough that the dev doesn't have to re-discover the architecture.

- `src/plan/adopt-diff.ts` (new) — `computeAdoptDiff(origin, plan)`. Case-insensitive, trimmed set diff of `[...origin.habitTitles, ...origin.topEatFoods]` against `[...plan.habitStack.habits.map(h => h.title), ...plan.eatList.map(e => e.food)]`. Return `{ kept, dropped, added }`. Reuse the comparison idiom the plan-delta surface (0029) already uses if one exists; otherwise a small local diff. Pure; no Dexie, no LLM.
- `src/types.ts` — add `AdoptOrigin { version: 1; habitTitles: string[]; topEatFoods: string[] }` and extend `Profile` with optional `adoptOrigin?: AdoptOrigin`. Additive. (`AdoptSeed` from 0020 already exists; `AdoptOrigin` is the retained, render-only twin — keep them distinct so the single-use clear of `adoptSeed` does not touch `adoptOrigin`.)
- `src/db.ts` — bump Dexie to the next version with a NEW additive `.version(N).stores({...})` block for the `adoptOrigin` field (per the AGENTS.md schema rule: never edit existing version blocks). Read-side optional; no data migration needed.
- `src/pages/plan.ts` — at the adopt-seeded first compose (the existing 0020 branch that clears `adoptSeed` after saving the plan), ALSO write `profile.adoptOrigin = { version: 1, habitTitles, topEatFoods }` from the seed if `adoptOrigin` is not already set — set-once. Then render the "What you kept and changed" section when `profile.adoptOrigin` is present and a plan exists, calling `computeAdoptDiff(profile.adoptOrigin, latestPlan())`. Add the "Share back" action wired to the share-back composer.
- `src/share/share-protocol.ts` (0017) / the tell-a-friend path (0050) — read-only reuse. Build the `#/shared` link via 0017's existing encoder and invoke 0050's native-share (with its clipboard fallback). The share-back message prepends the kept/dropped/added summary to the existing share text; do NOT invent a second share mechanism or a second link encoder.
- `src/claude.ts` — read-only confirm: `formatProfile()` (the intake-summary block 0020 wired) must NOT read `adoptOrigin`. Add a test asserting the prompt body never contains the origin's strings.
- `src/styles.css` — `.adopt-diff` (the section), `.adopt-diff__kept` / `--dropped` / `--added` (three short lists), reusing the plan-delta (0029) section idioms. "Share back" action ≥44×44 css px (`box-sizing: border-box; min-height: 45px`). Grid children `min-width: 0`. No new colors; ink and ink-soft, oxblood reserved for the share action.
- `src/sample/state.ts` / `src/share/shared-state.ts` — gate the section with `!isTour() && !isSharedView()` AND `profile.adoptOrigin` present. Neither mode fabricates an origin.
- `tests/helpers/flows.ts` — reuse `enterSharedViewWithAdopt` + `composePlan` (from 0020's spec) to drive a real adopt compose; add a `seedAdoptOrigin(page, origin)` helper to set `adoptOrigin` directly for the render-only tests without the full adopt round-trip. Reuse 0020's `lastPromptBody()` mock accessor for the never-re-prompted assertion and 0050's share-path assertions for the share-back.
- `tests/e2e/adopt-diff.spec.ts` (new) — every acceptance bullet maps to a `test()`. The retain-but-never-prompt test composes an adopt-seeded plan, asserts `adoptOrigin` persisted, recomposes, and asserts the prompt body excludes the origin strings; the share-back test taps the action and asserts the message carries the kept/dropped/added summary + a `#/shared` link and none of a sentinel marker value.
- `tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts` — extend with the adopt-diff render + share-back, confirming zero off-allow-list egress and no labs/insights/values/key in the message or link.
- New deps: **no** — reuse 0017's encoder and 0050's native-share.
- Egress allow-list change: **no**.
- Schema migration: **yes** — Dexie next version, additive `Profile.adoptOrigin?`.
- Voice spec: the section eyebrow reads "What you kept and changed"; the three lists are labeled "Kept", "Dropped", "Added". The share-back action reads "Share back". The message reads "I adopted your protocol — here's what stuck for me, and what was different:". Declarative, no exclamation, no naming the friend. Banned: "amazing," "exciting," "journey," "great."
