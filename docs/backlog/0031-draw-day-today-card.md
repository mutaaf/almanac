## User story

As a user who, after seeing the retest nudge, finally booked the draw — and whose appointment is *this morning* — I want the Today page to recognize that today is draw day and meet me there ("Today is your draw day. Fasting markers read truest before food and coffee — the lentil-and-egg breakfast can wait until after."), so that the surface I open every morning is not silent on the one day it matters most, and so the small but real things that change the read of a fasting panel — eat after, not before — arrive at the moment I can act on them, instead of as a regret I have after the needle is already out.

## Why now (four lenses)

### Product Owner

Today already adapts to context — it has a Sunday recap (0008), a quiet-day card (0015), and a lapse-aware welcome-back (0018). It does not adapt to the single most important date in the whole retest loop: the day the user's blood is actually drawn. When draw day arrives, the app is silent, even though it knows (or could know) the date — the retest surface (0028) computes a due date, and a user who acts on that nudge has a concrete appointment. The smallest meaningful unit of value is a draw-day branch on Today: if the user has marked a draw date (a single, optional "I booked my draw for —" affordance the retest card can offer, persisted locally) and today is that date, Today leads with a calm draw-day card carrying the two or three things that actually change a panel's read — fasting-marker timing (eat and caffeinate *after*, not before), hydration, and a one-line "when the results are in, they go here" link to labs. It is a small, high-trust touch that makes the app feel like it is *with* the user on the day that counts. It depends on 0028 for the due-date plumbing but ships its own self-contained value.

### Stakeholder

This deepens the **adherence loop** and the **persistent timeline** at their most fragile seam: the hand-off from "the plan said retest" to "a new panel exists." That seam is where the loop most often breaks — the user means to retest, books it, and then either eats breakfast and fouls a fasting marker, or simply forgets the app is even part of the day. A draw-day card converts the abstract retest cadence into presence on the actual day, which raises the odds that the draw happens *well* (fasting respected) and that its results come back into the timeline. It is a small surface that protects a large investment: every panel the timeline ever holds had a draw day, and the quality of that draw determines the quality of every insight built on it. A backend product would email a reminder the night before; we put the right card on the surface the user already opens, computed from a date they own, with no notification permission and no server. It is the calm, local version of the appointment reminder — and the one that respects the privacy contract.

### User (at 7am on the phone)

The morning of the draw. I open Today out of habit, before I have eaten. The page leads with a quiet card: "Today is your draw day. Fasting markers read truest before food and coffee — hold breakfast until after the needle. Drink water; it makes the draw easier. When the results are in, they go here." It is exactly the nudge I needed and would not have remembered on my own — I was about to make coffee. I close the app and go. Later, results in hand, I tap the card's link and upload them. The whole thing is two calm sentences at the right moment. Setting it up was one tap on the retest card last week ("book your draw → pick a date"). It works offline, instantly, because the draw date is on my phone and the card is computed locally — no call, no permission prompt, no alarm.

### Growth

This is a low-cost, high-affection retention touch — the kind of small correctness that makes a user trust the product with the next year of their health. Retention: the day the user retests is the day the loop either continues or quietly ends; an app that shows up *well* on that day (and helps the draw go right) earns the second, third, fourth panel that turns a tourist into a resident. (Hypothesis: a draw-day card that protects fasting-marker timing reduces "fouled" repeat panels and raises the rate of clean second draws entering the timeline; and the affection generated by "the app knew it was my draw day and reminded me not to eat" is a disproportionate source of word-of-mouth relative to its build cost — treat as untested without remote analytics.) The "show me" moment is small but real: "it told me not to eat before my blood draw" is the kind of specific, caring detail a user mentions to a friend unprompted, because it is the opposite of the generic SaaS nudge — it is the product paying attention to *their* week. Affection compounds into referral more reliably than features do.

## Acceptance criteria

Each box maps 1:1 to a Playwright test scenario.

- [ ] The retest card (0028) gains an optional "Book your draw" affordance that opens a single date input and, on confirm, persists a `drawDate` locally (a small record in IndexedDB tied to the latest plan's retest, or a dedicated tiny table — confirm the cleanest local home in `src/db.ts`). With no draw date set, behavior is exactly as 0028 ships. Asserted by setting a draw date from the retest card and confirming it persists across a reload.
- [ ] `src/today/draw-day.ts` (new) exports `isDrawDay(drawDate: Date | null, now: Date): boolean` — true only when `drawDate` is set and falls on the same calendar day as `now` (local date comparison, not UTC). Pure: no DOM, no Dexie, no network. Asserted with a draw date equal to today (true), yesterday (false), tomorrow (false), and null (false).
- [ ] On the Today page (`src/pages/today.ts`), when `isDrawDay(...)` is true, a draw-day card renders at the TOP of the Today body, above the day's meals. On any other day the card is absent and Today is byte-for-byte unchanged. Asserted by seeding a draw date of today (card present) versus tomorrow (card absent).
- [ ] The card reads exactly: "Today is your draw day. Fasting markers read truest before food and coffee — hold breakfast until after. Drink water; it makes the draw easier. When the results are in, they go here." with "they go here" as the single tap to `#/labs`. Asserted against the rendered copy and the navigation on tap.
- [ ] The draw-day card and the retest due card (0028) do NOT both show on the same day — on draw day, the draw-day card supersedes the retest nudge (the draw is happening; no need to also say it is due). Asserted by seeding a cadence that is both due and whose draw date is today, confirming only the draw-day card renders.
- [ ] After the user uploads a panel drawn on the draw date (or later), the stored `drawDate` is considered satisfied and the draw-day card no longer fires for it (the draw happened and the results arrived). Asserted by setting today as draw day, adding a panel drawn today, and confirming the card clears on the next render.
- [ ] The user can clear or change a set draw date (the "Book your draw" affordance shows the current date and allows editing/removing it). Asserted by setting, then editing, then removing the draw date and confirming the card state tracks each change.
- [ ] The card is computed with **zero network calls** — it reads the stored draw date and `now`. Asserted by rendering Today on draw day and confirming the mock recorded zero requests.
- [ ] Renders on both **chromium and mobile-webkit**. The card and its link are ≥44 css px touch targets; the copy wraps cleanly at the 390 px viewport; the date input is usable on mobile-webkit (native date picker). Note in the spec which input mechanism is asserted per project.
- [ ] Tour mode (0014) renders the draw-day card only if the tour fixture sets a draw date of "today" (otherwise absent — never fabricated). Shared-view mode (0017) does NOT render it — the surface is the owner's Today. Asserted in both modes.
- [ ] Privacy E2E (`tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts`) still passes — the draw date is local-only; no new hostnames; no inference call; no notification/permission API is invoked.
- [ ] Schema migration: **yes if a new table** — if the draw date needs a new Dexie store, bump the schema version with a new `.version(N).stores({...})` block (never edit existing ones) and add a migration test that confirms an existing DB upgrades cleanly with no data loss. If the draw date can live on the existing plan/retest record, prefer that and note **no** migration. Confirm which in `src/db.ts` before coding.
- [ ] New `tests/e2e/draw-day.spec.ts` covers: book-draw persistence across reload, `isDrawDay` same-day logic, card present on draw day and absent otherwise, exact copy + tap-to-labs, draw-day card supersedes the retest card, card clears after a covering draw, edit/remove the draw date, zero-network, tour vs shared-view behavior, mobile rendering, and (if a new table) the migration test.

## Out of scope

Explicit anti-goals. The dev agent will not do these even if they seem related.

- Push notifications, web-push, calendar (.ics) export, or any reminder that fires outside the app. The card lives on Today, on the day, computed locally. We do not request notification permission and we do not write to the OS calendar.
- Booking the draw with a lab or syncing an appointment from a calendar provider. We have no backend and no integration; the user enters the date they already have. The affordance is a local date input, nothing more.
- A full appointments / scheduling surface or recurring-draw automation. One optional draw date tied to the current retest, set by one tap. Recurring schedules are gold-plating.
- Medical fasting instructions beyond the calm, general line in the card. We are not a clinician; the card states the widely-understood "fasting markers read truer before food" and "hydrate," with the standing disclaimer. No marker-specific fasting-hour prescriptions, no "fast for exactly 12 hours" medical instruction.
- Shipping this before 0028. The draw-day card reads the retest plumbing 0028 establishes (the due-date computation and the retest card that offers "book your draw"). 0028 is the prerequisite; this ticket is the self-contained day-of layer on top. If 0028 has not shipped, this ticket waits — but it remains a single shippable unit once 0028 lands, not a multi-phase plan.
- Carrying the draw date into any static artifact (PDF 0010/0016, share link 0017, Markdown export 0027). It is a live, day-of, on-device surface.

## Engineering notes

Files / patterns the dev should touch. Be specific enough that the dev doesn't have to re-discover the architecture.

- `src/today/draw-day.ts` (new) — `isDrawDay(drawDate, now)` per the acceptance bullets, comparing local calendar dates (year/month/day in local time, not `Date` equality or UTC). Pure.
- `src/db.ts` — decide the home for `drawDate`. Cleanest options, in order of preference: (a) a field on the existing plan/retest record if the schema permits an additive field without a store change; (b) a tiny dedicated single-row store (`drawDates` keyed by plan id) added via a new `.version(N).stores({...})` block — never edit existing version blocks. Add the read/write helpers (`setDrawDate`, `getDrawDate`, `clearDrawDate`) here. If a new store, add a migration test per the acceptance criteria.
- `src/pages/today.ts` — the page already branches for the quiet-day (0015), recap (0008), lapse (0018), and (with 0028) retest surfaces. Add the draw-day branch at the top of the adaptive region, above meals, gated on `isDrawDay(getDrawDate(...), new Date())`, and make it supersede the 0028 retest card on the same day. Re-use the existing adaptive-card idioms.
- `src/today/retest.ts` (from 0028) — extend the retest card render to offer the "Book your draw" affordance (a date input that calls `setDrawDate`). Keep the affordance optional; 0028's behavior with no draw date set is unchanged.
- The "card clears after a covering draw" logic — read `allPanels()` and check for a panel drawn on/after the `drawDate`; if present, the draw is satisfied. Re-use the same panel-loading helper 0028 uses.
- `src/types.ts` — add `DrawDate` (if a dedicated record) and any internal render type. Additive.
- `src/styles.css` — `.draw-day-card` matching the existing Today adaptive cards; ink and ink-soft only, no oxblood (this is calm, not an alert). The date input styled to match the app's form inputs.
- `src/share/shared-state.ts` / `src/sample/state.ts` — gate with `isSharedView()` (never) / `isTour()` (only if the fixture sets today as draw day).
- `tests/helpers/flows.ts` — add a `setDrawDate(page, date)` helper that drives the book-draw affordance (or writes the date to Dexie directly for the cards that only need the state). Drive `now` via Playwright's clock API or relative seeding so "today / yesterday / tomorrow" are deterministic.
- `tests/e2e/draw-day.spec.ts` (new) — every acceptance bullet maps to a `test()`. If a new store is added, the migration test seeds a pre-version DB (or the prior version's data) and asserts a clean upgrade.
- `tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts` — extend with a draw-day Today visit confirming zero off-allow-list egress and that no notification/permission API is invoked.
- New deps: **no**.
- Egress allow-list change: **no**.
- Schema migration: **yes only if a dedicated store is chosen** — bump the Dexie version with a new block, add a migration test. Prefer an additive field on an existing record to avoid the bump if the schema allows it.
- Voice spec: the card reads "Today is your draw day. Fasting markers read truest before food and coffee — hold breakfast until after. Drink water; it makes the draw easier. When the results are in, they go here." Declarative, no exclamation, no alarm. Banned: "amazing," "exciting," "journey," "great." Ink and ink-soft only.
