## User story

As a user who just uploaded a new draw and saw the movement receipt (0032) tell me ferritin came up from 32 to 48 — now inside the functional range, I want to tap that line and see the whole chain behind it: the insight the engine flagged on the prior draw, the plan action(s) that targeted ferritin, my actual adherence to those actions over the months since, and the resulting delta — all on one page, deterministic, no chat — so that for the first time I can read the *causal lineage* of an improvement on my own data, and so that the answer to "did the protocol work?" stops being "the marker moved" and becomes "this insight named the problem, this action targeted it, you held it 18 of 21 days, and the marker moved this far."

## Why now (four lenses)

### Product Owner

The product has shipped the four moat assets — curated functional ranges, deterministic insight engine, structured plan with `markerKeys`-tagged actions, persistent adherence timeline — and a handful of surfaces that touch one or two of them at a time: 0032 (the marker moved) names the result, 0013 (insight provenance) shows the rule's evidence, 0034 (you earned the promotion) shows adherence promoting a habit, 0019 (what-if simulator) lets the user replay the insight on a hypothetical value, 0062 (what worked) gives a per-action audit at recompose. What does not yet exist is the surface that composes *all four* on one read, drilled into a single moved marker. The smallest meaningful unit of value is a "Lineage" sub-page reachable from any 0032 movement-receipt line and from the marker deep-dive (0059, when it ships) — `#/progress/lineage/:markerKey`. For the named marker it deterministically assembles, with NO LLM call: (1) the insight(s) from the prior plan whose `markerKeys` include this marker, with one editorial sentence each from the curated rule library (which 0013 already exposes); (2) the plan action(s) — eat-list items, habits, supplements, lifestyle — from the prior plan with this `markerKey`; (3) the adherence to each habit and to each eat-list food, computed from `CheckIn.habitsCompleted` and `CheckIn.mealsAte` over the window between the prior draw and the new draw; (4) the marker's start, end, and delta (the same `MarkerMove` shape 0032 produces). One page, four sections, deterministic, offline. Nothing new is persisted; nothing new is inferred at render time. It is the single-marker drill-down complement to 0062's whole-plan retrospective audit.

### Stakeholder

This is the most direct *moat-deepening* surface the product can ship inside its constraints, because it is the single page that is structurally impossible for a chat box to produce. A chat box does not have a prior plan's insights stored, does not have the action-to-marker-key map, does not have the per-day adherence timeline tied to the same plan's habits and meal ids, and does not have a curated functional range to anchor the delta against. Even with the entire conversation history pasted in, the chat box cannot honestly answer "and what did I actually do" because that data does not exist outside the device. The lineage page makes the moat visible: the product is the only place this read can exist, and the read is precisely the read the persona wanted from the start — "show me that the food moved my biology, and show me what part of the food I actually ate." It compounds every prior ticket: 0013's rule-evidence library, 0029's plan-delta logic, 0032's movement receipt, 0034's adherence-into-promotion mechanic, 0053's habit history, the meal-id tally 0008 / 0035 already use, and 0062's per-action audit. None of those pieces alone is the lineage; the composition is. It is also the most powerful possible answer to the readme's anti-wrapper argument — a screenshot of the lineage IS the wrapper-vs-product comparison, on the user's own marker.

### User (at 7am on the phone)

A new draw landed yesterday; today's morning open shows the 0032 receipt on the labs review screen — "Ferritin came up from 32 to 48 ng/mL — now inside the functional range." I tap it. A new page reads like a small dated essay on one marker. "Iron-restricted erythropoiesis — flagged on the February panel: ferritin 32 with normal hemoglobin pointed to depleting iron stores." Below: "Plan actions targeting ferritin: leafy greens daily, cook in cast iron, lentils 3× weekly." Below that: "How you actually held it: leafy greens 71% of days, cast iron 84%, lentils 19 of 21 dinners that called for them." And at the bottom, the marker line itself: "Ferritin: 32 → 48 ng/mL, +16, crossed into the functional range on May 24." I can read the whole chain on one page, every sentence dated, none of it a model speaking — it is my own data, arranged in the order that answers the question I actually had. It worked offline, instantly, on a phone, hands wet, because every byte was already on my phone. The first thing I want to do is screenshot it and send it to the friend who told me "everything looks normal but it isn't."

### Growth

This is a moat-deepening *and* retention-bearing ticket whose audience is the engaged resident who has stayed long enough for a marker to move — the highest-LTV cohort the product has. (Hypothesis: a lineage page rendered on every favorable move materially increases the rate at which a user who reaches their second draw reaches a third, because the second-draw payoff is now the strongest possible proof the loop works on the user's own data, and because the page is the most forwardable artifact the product can produce — a friend looking at a screenshot of "the insight, the action, the adherence, the result" understands what the product is doing in one read, where a feature tour or a sample plan asks them to imagine it; back-of-envelope from how outcome-attribution surfaces retain users in habit and fitness apps versus surfaces that only show the outcome; treat as untested without remote analytics, which we will never add.) The "show me" frame is the page itself, and it is the strongest one the product has — not because we engineered virality but because the artifact *is* the argument. It is the single-marker drill-down sibling of 0062 (which gives the per-action audit across the whole prior plan): 0062 answers "which bets paid off," this answers "what was the full chain behind the one that did."

## Out of scope

Explicit anti-goals. The dev agent will not do these even if they seem related.

- An LLM-composed narrative ("here is the story of your ferritin"). Every sentence on the page is deterministic — insight text from the curated rule library, action text from the persisted plan, adherence percentages from the check-in store, marker delta from the panel pair. No inference at render time. No new Anthropic call.
- Causal inference beyond the structural "the plan targeted this marker via this action; here is your adherence; here is the move." The page does NOT claim the actions caused the move; it composes the four pieces and lets the user read the chain. The voice is reportorial, not causal — "the plan asked, you held it, the marker moved," never "your magnesium caused this."
- Surfacing the lineage as a share card / image artifact. The lineage is text. An image variant is the territory of 0011 (marker hero) / 0047 (improvement card) — they can be extended to optionally reference the lineage in a future ticket, but no image render here.
- A lineage across more than two draws (the longitudinal arc of one marker over four panels). This ticket is the lineage between two adjacent draws of a moved marker. Multi-draw lineage is a plausible future ticket (sibling to 0042 the protocol-over-time view) — keep this one focused.
- A lineage for a marker that did NOT move. The acquisition value of this surface depends on the move being real; the no-move case is the territory of the 0039 ruled-out surface and the 0032 "still inside / unchanged" note. The empty-state branch already handles "we need two draws," not "we have two draws but nothing changed" — document the chosen handling in the spec.
- Duplicating 0062's per-action audit. 0062 lists every prior action across the whole plan and tags each with its measured outcome; this lineage drills into ONE marker and shows the four-section composition (insight, actions, adherence, result) for that marker alone. The two are siblings; the lineage is not a superset of 0062 and 0062 is not a superset of the lineage.
- Editing the actions, the adherence, or the marker values from the lineage page. The page is read-only over the persisted timeline; edits happen on their existing surfaces.
- Carrying the lineage into the doctor PDF (0010/0016), the Markdown export (0027), or the coach packet (0048). The coach packet is the right home for an exported version of this chain — but that is the work of 0048, not this ticket. Here the lineage is an in-app artifact.
- A second persistence path or a new store. The lineage is a pure read over existing stores. No Dexie schema change.
