## User story

As a prospect who landed on the pre-key marker reference (0038), scrolled to ApoB, and recognized the marker my friend's doctor never ordered, I want a one-tap "Share this marker" affordance on each reference row that pre-composes a plain-text message naming the marker, its functional optimum versus the lab range, the curated one-line rationale, and a link back to the public reference, so that the friend who has been told "everything's normal" lands on the same page with that one marker already in view — instead of me having to copy-paste five fields and explain what a functional range is in my own words.

## Why now (four lenses)

### Product Owner

The functional-range reference (0038, shipped) is the most knowledge-dense pre-key acquisition surface the product has — seventy markers with both ranges and a curated rationale, browsable with no key, no signup, no IndexedDB read, no inference. Today it converts the *reader*: the prospect who skims it forms an opinion of the curation and decides to set up. What it does NOT do is let that reader *forward* a single marker to a specific person who would care about that specific number — the friend who has been told their lipids are fine but never had an ApoB, the partner who is exhausted and never had a ferritin. The reference is a library; libraries are useful when you can hand someone a single page. The smallest meaningful unit of value is a restrained "Share" affordance on each reference row (or on a tap-to-expand row state — pick one and document) that composes a pure-ASCII plain-text message naming the marker, its unit, its lab range, its functional optimum, the curated `description`, and the canonical reference URL with a fragment id pointing to that marker's section (`#/reference#<markerKey>`) so the recipient lands with that marker scrolled into view. On mobile it opens `navigator.share({ text })` — the OS share sheet (Messages, WhatsApp, wherever); on desktop where `navigator.share` is absent it falls back to clipboard copy with a transient "Copied." It also writes the marker key as a URL fragment so the reference page, on receiving `#/reference#apob`, scrolls the ApoB row into view and applies a subtle highlight for a moment. It is a pure render of static data already in the bundle — no key, no IndexedDB, no Anthropic call, no widening of the egress allow-list (the canonical URL is the SPA's own origin).

### Stakeholder

This makes the **curated functional-range DB** — already the most credible, knowledge-dense top-of-funnel artifact the product owns — viral at the *single-marker* granularity that is closest to a real conversation. Today, the persona links someone to `#/reference`, the friend opens it, sees seventy markers, and bounces — too much library, no entry point. With per-marker share, the persona forwards exactly one marker that is exactly relevant to the friend's exact complaint — and the friend lands on that marker with the curation right there, on the page, no signup needed to read it. It is the structural opposite of a chat box: Claude can paste a paragraph about ApoB, but it cannot hand a friend a deep-link to a curated, source-grounded, dual-range entry in a reference book that the friend can keep open and skim around. It compounds the shipped acquisition stack tightly: 0038 (reference) is the library, 0040 (sandbox) is "score your own numbers," 0049 (handoff) is the conversion close — this ticket puts the library's individual pages into the share sheet so the funnel starts with the most specific possible entry point (one marker, the right one). And it stays inside the privacy contract: the share payload carries no user data — there is no user data on the reference page to begin with (it reads no IndexedDB, has no profile, no key, no panels) — the message is one curated, public-domain marker entry, and the URL is the SPA's own origin with a fragment. Nothing personal leaves any device.

### User (at 7am on the phone)

A friend's labs came back "normal" again and she is still exhausted; I am on the reference page reading. I tap ApoB — the row expands, or a tiny "Share" link appears beside it. I tap it. The OS share sheet comes up with a message already written: "ApoB — atherogenic-particle count, the better heart-risk read than LDL cholesterol. Lab range up to 130 mg/dL; functional optimum under 90. Worth asking your doctor about it." Underneath it, a link to the same page she will land on, scrolled to ApoB. I send it to her in Messages. She taps it, lands on `#/reference#apob`, sees the row highlighted, and reads the curation in the same calm editorial voice. No signup, no chatbot answer, no forum thread — one marker, one page, one specific reason to look at it. If she scrolls, she finds sixty-nine other curated markers and the path to the sandbox and the tour. It worked offline because the entire library shipped with the page; the only thing that travelled was a sentence and a same-origin URL.

### Growth

This is the most forwardable artifact the product has, because it operates at the granularity of an actual conversation — one marker, one friend, one specific complaint — instead of the granularity of a product pitch (a tour, a sandbox, a feature list). (Hypothesis: a per-marker share affordance materially raises the rate at which a reference-page reader forwards anything at all, because the cost of forwarding "this is the marker you should ask about" with the curation pre-composed is roughly zero, where the cost of forwarding the whole reference and explaining what to look at is high enough that most readers do not bother; and the friend who receives a single-marker pre-composed message converts at a higher rate than one who receives a generic product link, because the entry is keyed to their actual complaint and the surface they land on is substantive rather than promotional; back-of-envelope from how "share this section" deep-links outperform "share the article" links in long-form reference content; treat as untested without remote analytics, which we will never add.) The "show me" frame is the share-sheet preview — a clean editorial line with the marker name, both ranges, the curated rationale, and a same-origin URL — which is exactly the kind of substance-not-pitch artifact the literate persona forwards unprompted. It compounds 0038 directly (the reference is the moat; the share button advertises one row of it at a time, where conversations actually happen), 0040 and 0055 indirectly (the friend who lands on the marker has the sandbox and paste-your-labs one tap away from the same page), and the share-with-a-friend pattern (0050, shipped, the Plan-page peer share) at the pre-key tier of the funnel — same mechanic, same one-tap discipline, same wordmark-carries-the-brand approach, at the top of the funnel instead of mid-protocol.

## Out of scope

Explicit anti-goals. The dev agent will not do these even if they seem related.

- A QR code on the share message. Same constraint as 0050's "Tell a friend": the share is a plain-text message; QR adds an image-render dependency and a scan step that is worse than a tappable same-origin URL.
- A link-shortener integration (Bitly, tinyurl, anything). AGENTS.md forbids the backend; a shortener adds a third-party host to the egress allow-list and a tracking layer the product refuses on principle. The canonical URL is the SPA's own `location.origin` plus the hash fragment.
- Tracking which markers were shared. There are no analytics in the product (AGENTS.md non-negotiable). "Most-shared marker" is not measured, not stored, not surfaced. The share affordance fires and forgets.
- An LLM-generated personalized share message ("here's why I think *you* should ask about ApoB"). The composed text is a deterministic render of static curated content; a Claude call would add cost, latency, non-determinism, an egress, and — critically — require a key on a page that is explicitly pre-key. The reference page has no key.
- Carrying the share affordance into the gated parts of the app (Labs, Progress, Plan, marker-detail panel from 0059). 0050 already covers the gated Plan-page peer share for the user's *own* protocol; 0011 covers the marker-hero image card for the user's own current value. This ticket scopes to the pre-key reference page, which is the substance-forward acquisition surface. The marker-detail panel (0059) may add its own share affordance later; that is its decision, not this ticket's.
- A "view in marker detail" CTA on the recipient's landing page that requires consent + onboarding. The friend who lands on `#/reference#apob` lands on the public reference page — the existing "Start your own" CTA already routes them to `#/welcome` if they want to set up. The fragment scroll is a reading affordance, not a funnel coercion.
- Pre-filling the share message with the recipient's name or with the sender's profile name. The reference page has no profile context (it reads no IndexedDB) and adding sender personalization would require a profile read, which 0038's privacy contract forbids on this page. The message is universal.
- An RSS/sitemap that exposes per-marker URLs to search engines. The reference is an SPA route; the hash fragment is client-side state. If discoverability via search is wanted later, that is a separate, larger SEO design that would need static-rendering plumbing the product does not have today.
- A `<meta property="og:..."` OpenGraph injection per marker for richer share-sheet previews. The SPA cannot per-marker per-fragment update OG metas without server-side rendering (no backend). The plain-text message is the share artifact; a rich preview is a follow-up only if the share rate justifies the SSR cost (which it almost certainly will not).
