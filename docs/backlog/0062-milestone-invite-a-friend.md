## User story

As a user whose ferritin just crossed from 28 into the functional band on my latest draw — the first hard proof the protocol is working — I want Today to quietly acknowledge that win and offer, right there, a one-tap "tell a friend" that hands my phone's share sheet a message already written from my own result ("My ferritin went from 28 to 54 in three months on a food-first protocol — this is the app I used: <tour link>"), so that in the rare, real moment I actually feel like recommending this, the share is one tap away and already says the true, specific thing — instead of me having to think to go find a share button on a different page and write the message myself.

## Why now (four lenses)

### Product Owner

The product already has the pieces of a referral but not the *moment*. Tell-a-friend (0050, shipped) is an always-available share button on Plan — good, but it sits on a page the user visits deliberately, and it fires whenever the user happens to press it, not when they actually feel like recommending. The marker-moved receipt (0032, shipped) already detects the high-affect event: a new draw lands a number where the protocol aimed it. The smallest meaningful unit of value is to connect those two — when a draw moves a marker into (or toward) its functional band, Today surfaces a calm, dismissible card that names the win in the user's own numbers and offers a one-tap invite whose message is *pre-composed from that specific improvement*: "My <marker> went from <old> to <new> in <span> on a food-first protocol — here's the app: <tour link>". On phones that expose `navigator.share` it opens the OS share sheet; otherwise it copies the message. It carries no labs, no personal data beyond the one marker delta the user chooses to send, and links to the existing pre-consent tour (0014), never into the user's data. It is the right offer at the right moment, built from primitives that already exist.

### Stakeholder

This is the cleanest acquisition wedge the product has, because it fires referral at the one instant referral actually converts: the moment of demonstrated, personal proof. A precision-health app's most credible advertisement is not a feature list — it is a real person saying "my number moved." The marker-moved event (0032) is exactly that, and it is a thing only this product can detect, because only a persistent-timeline, functional-range app *knows* that a new draw crossed the optimum threshold the protocol set. A chat box has no draw history to compare against and no functional band to cross, so it has no "your marker moved" moment to hang an invite on; the moment is structurally a feature of the moat. The shared message — the user's own before/after delta plus the tour link — is the most forwardable artifact the funnel has because it is true, specific, and personal, and it carries nothing private (one marker delta the user opts to send, no key, no labs, no link into the data). It compounds the funnel the tour (0014), the sample tour, and the reference (0038) already feed: a warm, named prospect arriving on a credible "her ferritin doubled" rather than a cold ad.

### User (at 7am on the phone)

A new draw is in, and Today acknowledges it without fuss: "Your ferritin moved into range — 28 to 54 since February." Below it, one quiet line: "Want to tell someone?" I do, actually — my sister has the same dismissed-as-normal labs. I tap it. The share sheet comes up with the message already written, in my numbers: "My ferritin went from 28 to 54 in three months on a food-first protocol — this is the app I used." I pick her, send, done. I did not have to find a share page, did not have to compose anything, did not have to decide what to say — the app caught me in the one moment I felt like recommending it and made the share a single tap. If I do not feel like it, I dismiss the card and it does not nag me again for this win. Nothing of mine left the phone but the one sentence I chose to send.

### Growth

This is a pure acquisition ticket built on the highest-conversion referral moment a health product has: proof of a personal result. (Hypothesis: a milestone-triggered, pre-composed invite converts to a sent referral at a higher rate than an always-on share button, because it fires at the exact moment the user feels recommending impulse and removes the compose friction entirely — the user need only choose a recipient; back-of-envelope from how moment-triggered, pre-filled referral prompts outperform passive share buttons in consumer apps, and from how demonstrated personal results outperform feature pitches in health word-of-mouth; treat as untested without remote analytics, which we will never add.) The "show me" frame is the message itself: "my ferritin went from 28 to 54 in three months — here's the app" is the single most persuasive line the product can put in front of a cold prospect, because it is a real person's real number. It is the moment-triggered sibling of tell-a-friend (0050): 0050 is the share you press when you decide to; this is the share offered at the moment you most want to.

## Acceptance criteria

Each box maps 1:1 to a Playwright test scenario.

- [ ] `src/today/milestone-invite.ts` (new) exports `detectMarkerMilestone(panels: Panel[], plan: Plan | undefined): { markerKey: string; name: string; from: number; to: number; span: string } | null`. It detects, deterministically and with NO network/LLM, the most recent draw that moved a marker INTO (or measurably toward — pick one and document the rule) its functional band relative to the prior draw of the same marker, returning the marker's name, the prior value, the new value, and a human span between the two draws ("three months", computed from `drawnAt`). Returns `null` when no such crossing exists. Reuses the same crossing logic the marker-moved receipt (0032) already established; do not invent a second definition. Pure: no DOM, no Dexie, no network. Asserted by seeding two draws that cross the band (returns the delta) and two that do not (returns null).
- [ ] `src/today/milestone-invite.ts` also exports `composeInviteMessage(m: MarkerMilestone, tourUrl: string): string` building the pre-composed message in the user's own numbers — e.g. "My <name> went from <from> to <to> in <span> on a food-first protocol — here's the app I used: <tourUrl>". The message contains the marker delta and the tour link and NOTHING else personal (no key, no other markers, no labs). Asserted by composing a message for a known milestone and confirming the delta, span, and tour URL appear and no other marker/personal value does.
- [ ] The Today page (`src/pages/today.ts`) renders a calm, dismissible "moment worth sharing" card when `detectMarkerMilestone(...)` is non-null AND the milestone has not already been dismissed (per the documented per-milestone dismiss key). The card names the win in the user's numbers and offers a one-tap invite. Asserted by seeding a band-crossing draw and confirming the card renders with the user's delta.
- [ ] Tapping the invite uses `navigator.share({ text })` on browsers that expose it (the share sheet receives `composeInviteMessage(...)`); on browsers without it, the affordance copies the same message to the clipboard and confirms "Copied". Asserted by stubbing `navigator.share` and asserting the shared text, and by asserting the clipboard fallback when `navigator.share` is absent — mirroring the 0050 / 0030 share-or-copy pattern exactly.
- [ ] The shared/copied message links ONLY to the existing pre-consent tour (0014) URL — never to the user's data, never a link that exposes the profile, key, or labs. Asserted by confirming the message URL is the tour link and contains no personal token.
- [ ] Dismissing the card hides it for THAT milestone and it does not reappear for the same crossing (the dismiss is keyed to the specific marker + draw, persisted locally — document the store/key). A LATER, different band-crossing on a future draw surfaces a fresh card. Asserted by dismissing, reloading (card absent), then seeding a new crossing (a fresh card appears).
- [ ] The card is ABSENT when no marker has crossed its band, when there is only one draw (no prior to compare), and when there is no composed plan. The existing Today page is unchanged in those states. Asserted on a single-draw profile and a no-plan profile.
- [ ] The card and the invite make **zero Anthropic calls** — the milestone is a deterministic read of the local `panels` and `plan`, and the share/copy is an OS/clipboard action. The ONLY network the message references is the pre-consent tour URL, which is on the existing allow-list (the app's own origin). Asserted by rendering the card and invoking the invite and confirming the mock recorded zero Anthropic requests and no off-allow-list egress.
- [ ] The Anthropic key NEVER appears in the card, the composed message, or any intermediate string. Asserted by seeding a sentinel key and confirming it appears nowhere in the card or the shared/copied text.
- [ ] Renders on both **chromium and mobile-webkit**: the card fits the 390 px viewport, the invite and dismiss affordances are ≥44×44 css px tap targets (min-height ≥45 px per the 0017 sub-pixel lesson), and a long marker name respects `min-width: 0`. Asserted on both projects.
- [ ] Tour mode (0014) and shared-view mode (0017) do NOT render the card (neither has a real draw history that crossed a band; the milestone detector returns null against fixture/shared data, and the card must not render even if seeded). Asserted in both modes.
- [ ] The existing Today surfaces — the daily check-in, the meal cards, the streak strip, the recap (0008), the retest/quiet-day/pre-draw/draw-day cards (0028/0015/0051/0031), the marker-moved receipt (0032) — are unchanged in behavior; the invite card is purely additive at the documented slot. Asserted by exercising the existing Today flows and confirming they are unchanged, and that the card does not displace or duplicate the 0032 receipt.
- [ ] Privacy E2E (`tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts`) still passes — the card is a local read; the invite is an OS share / clipboard action referencing only the tour URL; no new hostnames.
- [ ] Schema migration: **yes IF** the per-milestone dismiss needs persistence beyond an existing store — prefer reusing an existing dismiss/seen mechanism (the recap-dismiss or quiet-day-dismiss pattern, or a small localStorage key, mirroring how sibling Today cards persist dismissal). If a Dexie field is genuinely needed, bump additively per the AGENTS.md rule and add a migration test; otherwise note **no** and document the chosen dismiss store.
- [ ] New `tests/e2e/milestone-invite.spec.ts` covers: `detectMarkerMilestone` fires on a band-crossing and is null otherwise; `composeInviteMessage` carries only the delta + span + tour link; the card renders on a crossing and is dismissible per-milestone; a later crossing re-surfaces; `navigator.share` receives the message and the clipboard fallback works; absent on single-draw / no-plan; zero Anthropic + zero off-allow-list egress; the key never appears (sentinel); tour/shared-view absence; the existing Today flows unchanged; mobile rendering.

## Out of scope

Explicit anti-goals. The dev agent will not do these even if they seem related.

- A new always-on share button on Today. That is tell-a-friend (0050) on Plan. This card is *moment-triggered* — it appears only when a marker crosses its band, and it is dismissible. Do not add a permanent Today share control.
- Generating a phone-shaped IMAGE of the win to share. That is the improvement card (0047), a separate growth artifact. This ticket shares a pre-composed TEXT message via the OS share sheet / clipboard; linking to or reusing 0047's image is fine if trivial, building an image generator here is not.
- An LLM call to write the invite message. The message is a deterministic template filled with the user's own delta and the tour link. No inference, no Anthropic call, no per-share variation.
- Sharing more than the single marker delta the milestone names. The message carries one marker's before/after and the tour link — never the full panel, never other markers, never the key or any link into the user's data. Multi-marker or "share my whole win" framing is out of scope and a privacy risk.
- A backend referral system — invite tracking, referral codes, "you've referred N friends", reward credits. There is no server; the invite is a fire-and-forget OS share, nothing is tracked, nothing is counted. AGENTS.md forbids the server side and the analytics it would need.
- A push notification or any reach-out about the milestone. The card appears only when the user opens the app, and it never nags — one dismiss per milestone and it is gone for that crossing.
- Re-defining what "crossing the band" means. Reuse the exact crossing definition the marker-moved receipt (0032) already established so the two surfaces agree; do not introduce a second, divergent threshold rule.
- Surfacing the card for a marker that worsened or for a non-crossing improvement that does not reach the band (unless the documented "toward the band" rule explicitly includes it). The default is the high-confidence, high-affect event: a marker that crossed into range.

## Engineering notes

Files / patterns the dev should touch. Be specific enough that the dev doesn't have to re-discover the architecture.

- `src/today/milestone-invite.ts` (new) — `detectMarkerMilestone(panels, plan)` and `composeInviteMessage(m, tourUrl)`. For detection, reuse the crossing logic the marker-moved receipt (0032) uses (find it in the 0032 module / `src/insights.ts` or wherever 0032 landed — GREP the code before trusting prose, per the 2026-05-23 LESSON) so the two surfaces share one definition; compute the human span from the two draws' `drawnAt`. For the message, a plain template string with the marker name, `from`, `to`, span, and the tour URL. Pure; no Dexie import, no `claude.ts` import.
- `src/types.ts` — add a `MarkerMilestone` interface (markerKey, name, from, to, span). Additive, internal render type — NOT a persisted schema change unless the dismiss key requires one (prefer not).
- `src/pages/today.ts` — render the card at the documented slot when `detectMarkerMilestone(allPanels(), latestPlan())` is non-null and the milestone is not dismissed. The invite handler builds the message via `composeInviteMessage` and calls `navigator.share` (feature-detected) or the clipboard fallback — reuse the exact share-or-copy helper 0050 and 0030 use; do not re-implement it. The dismiss handler writes the per-milestone dismiss key.
- `src/today/` (dismiss persistence) — reuse the existing per-card dismiss/seen mechanism (the recap 0008 / quiet-day 0015 dismissal pattern, or a small localStorage key); key the dismiss to the marker + the new draw's date so a later crossing re-surfaces. Document the store/key. Avoid a Dexie bump if an existing mechanism fits.
- `src/share/` (tour URL) — reuse the existing pre-consent tour (0014) URL constant; the message links only to that. Confirm the tour link is the same one 0050 already composes into its message.
- `src/db.ts` — read-only reuse: `allPanels()` and `latestPlan()` supply the inputs. Do NOT alter a store unless the dismiss genuinely needs it (prefer not).
- `src/styles.css` — `.milestone-invite` (the card), reusing the Today editorial card idioms (the 0032 receipt and 0028 retest card are templates). No new colors; ink, ink-soft, oxblood reserved for the in-range emphasis. Invite + dismiss affordances ≥44×44 (`box-sizing: border-box; min-height: 45px`). Grid children `min-width: 0`.
- `src/sample/state.ts` / `src/share/shared-state.ts` — gate with `!isTour() && !isSharedView()`; the detector returns null against fixture/shared data, and the card must not render in either mode.
- `tests/helpers/flows.ts` — reuse `seedPanel` / `seedPlan` (and the share-stub pattern from the 0050 / 0030 specs — stub `navigator.share`, assert the text; remove the stub to assert the clipboard fallback). Seed two draws of the same marker that cross the band, and a control pair that does not.
- `tests/e2e/milestone-invite.spec.ts` (new) — every acceptance bullet maps to a `test()`. The detection test seeds a crossing pair and asserts the delta; the share test stubs `navigator.share` and asserts the composed message; the re-surface test dismisses one milestone, reloads (absent), then seeds a new crossing and asserts a fresh card.
- `tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts` — extend with a Today visit carrying a band-crossing draw, rendering the card and invoking the invite, confirming zero off-allow-list egress.
- New deps: **no**.
- Egress allow-list change: **no** (the tour link is the app's own origin; the share is an OS action).
- Schema migration: **no** if an existing dismiss mechanism fits (prefer this); **yes, additive** only if a Dexie dismiss field is unavoidable — document the choice.
- Voice spec: the card eyebrow reads plainly — "Your <marker> moved into range"; the invite line reads "Want to tell someone?"; the composed message reads "My <marker> went from <from> to <to> in <span> on a food-first protocol — here's the app I used: <link>". Declarative, no exclamation, no hype. Banned: "amazing," "exciting," "journey," "great." Ink and ink-soft; oxblood for the in-range emphasis only.
